01 — Edge compute

Edge Computing.

Scalable on-board computing power, from compact edge deployments to full enterprise-grade rack configurations, enabling virtualisation, real-time analytics, and IT management at sea.

02 — Security

Built-in security.

WatchGuard UTM firewall included in every configuration: stateful firewall, IPS, application control, and web filtering. EDR and vSOC available as managed services through Sentrify.

03 — Connectivity

Any connectivity.

Interoperable with every satellite and GSM provider: Starlink, VSAT, OneWeb, LTE/5G, L-band. WatchGuard SD-WAN provides intelligent routing and automatic failover across all links.

04 — Crew welfare

Crew welfare.

The Crew Internet Café gives seafarers controlled internet access with individual accounts, an e-wallet supporting pre-pay and post-pay credits, and configurable data quotas, all managed remotely via abela.io.

05 — Telemetry

Real-time telemetry.

Abela pulse gives operators a live onboard view of connectivity, hardware health, and compliance. Shore teams get full fleet-wide visibility through abela.io from a single portal.

06 — Managed IT

abela IT.

Full IT management for your fleet. VM hosting, remote maintenance, compliance reports, and IT policy management — your vessel as a remote office.
