One Net builds, manages, and secures the digital infrastructure that keeps maritime and enterprise operations connected — from multi-orbit satellite connectivity to cybersecurity, managed IT, and fleet software, all unified on the Zwana Nexus edge platform.

Our story

We exist to make the sea as connected as the shore — giving every vessel and remote site the same reliable, secure infrastructure an office takes for granted.

Operating at sea has always meant compromise: intermittent connectivity, fragmented systems, and limited support thousands of miles from the nearest engineer. One Net was built to close that gap — integrating connectivity, cybersecurity, IT and software into a single, fleet-wide platform, managed end-to-end by people who understand maritime operations.

Today, from our headquarters in Cyprus and hubs across key maritime regions, our engineering and operations teams keep thousands of vessels online around the clock — so crews stay connected and operations never stop.
